Your brain under abuse

Chronic psychological abuse can produce real nervous-system changes: a hyper-reactive amygdala, a stressed hippocampus, and a tired prefrontal cortex. That is why “just leave” can feel impossible. It is not weakness. The brain can recover with safety, time, and trauma-informed support.

Yellow Rock: the same protection with polite wording when children or logistics require contact.

Trauma bonds

A trauma bond is attachment built by intermittent reinforcement plus a power imbalance. Name it, interrupt contact, keep a reality journal, grieve the fantasy, and rebuild with safe people. Returning more than once is common and not failure.

🚪

Leave safely

Leaving can be the most dangerous time. Do not announce the plan. Work with 1800RESPECT or a local DV service. Quietly gather IDs, money, meds, keys, and a destination.
